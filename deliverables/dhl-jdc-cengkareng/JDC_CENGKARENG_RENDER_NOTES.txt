LIMITATIONS
  - Voice-over is Kokoro-82M (on-device TTS, voice af_heart) run through the HyperFrames CLI. HyperFrames was used for TTS only;
    every scene is built in Remotion. A professional voice actor would sound more natural, and the VO files can be
    swapped in public/audio/vo/ before re-running scripts/mix.py.
  - Pronunciation was checked from Kokoro's phonemes (D-H-L, J-D-C, "My D H L Plus", "Cheng-ka-reng"). Speech
    recognition could not be run because model downloads are blocked in this environment.
  - No real smartphone-speaker listening test was possible here.
  - Supplied renders are 2-D images: the ribbon, folds and morphs are procedural (SVG and CSS 3-D). The box open-to-closed
    change is hidden under the ribbon sweep rather than morphed.
  - WhatsApp-style UI colours (green, dark theme) appear only inside the phone screen.
